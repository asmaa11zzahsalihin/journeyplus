The Story Behind Our Establishment

Founded in Cyberjaya, Malaysia

In late 2024, our founder Nur Syadiatul Najwa went on a trip to Thailand with a group of four university friends. What was supposed to be a relaxing getaway quickly turned into a headache every evening at dinner tables.

"One person paid for hotel rooms in Malaysian Ringgit, another covered dinner in Thai Baht, and someone else bought train tickets online in USD. At the end of every day, we spent hours with notebooks, phone calculators, and dynamic exchange rates trying to figure out who owed what."

To make matters worse, existing money apps added hidden foreign currency markup fees, and none of them catered to Muslim travelers who wanted transparent Shariah-compliant safekeeping and instant prayer direction indicators while navigating unfamiliar foreign cities.

That was the lightbulb moment. The team assembled at Tamarind Square, Cyberjaya, to build JOURNEY+: an all-in-one Shariah e-wallet combining Wadiah savings, spot FX currency conversion, automated group bill splitting, and faith-aligned travel tools under one single roof.
